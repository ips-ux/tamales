## 2026-11-14 - Events and ordering

- You can now add events in Admin, under Events. Each event has a link and a QR code for the kiosk and for phones.
- For each event, you can sell a fixed number of tamales as pre-orders, or approve each pre-order yourself.
- Customers can send a batch request for pickup or delivery. You set the delivery distances and fees in Settings, under Ordering.
- At an event, customers order on their phone or at the kiosk. Live POS chimes when an order comes in. You can pause phone orders and mark items sold out.
- Each customer gets a page that tracks the order. It buzzes and changes color when you mark the order ready.
- Each event has a prep sheet and a pickup list that you can print.
- Online payment comes in the next piece. Until then, customers pay with Venmo, Cash App or cash, and you mark the order paid.
- From you: create your first event, and add your Venmo and Cash App names in Settings.

## 2026-11-13 - V1 design agreed

- We agreed what the first full version of your app will do. Nothing is built yet. The work starts next.
- Customers will pre-order for an event or send a batch request. They will pay ahead with PayPal, so you cook for paid orders only.
- At an event, customers will order from their phone or from your kiosk. They can pay with PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or cash.
- Each customer will get a tracker page that tells them when their order is ready.
- You will change the photos, the tagline, your story and your policies yourself. You will also get a prep sheet and a pickup list for each event.
- Your sales records will stay safe. A mistake is fixed with a void or a refund note, and a sale is never erased. One time each week, the app will save a backup to your Google Drive.
- The app has no monthly cost. You pay for the website name, and PayPal keeps a small fee on each online sale.
- The work comes in 4 pieces: 1 Foundation, 2 Events and ordering, 3 Money, 4 Backups.
